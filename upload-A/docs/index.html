The studio

Interiors that feel inevitable — as if the house was always meant to look this way.

Jessica Elizabeth is an interior design studio for people who care how a home feels to live in, not only how it photographs. Every project starts with how you actually use your rooms: where the morning light lands, where the bags get dropped, who cooks and who sits at the island.

From there the whole picture is composed — layout, lighting, materials, joinery, furniture and the small objects that make a room yours — and followed through until the last piece is in place.

  • 01

    Light first

    Every plan begins with daylight and ends with a lighting scheme for the evening.

  • 02

    Honest materials

    Stone, timber, plaster and natural textiles, chosen to age well rather than date quickly.

  • 03

    One point of contact

    We coordinate the trades, the orders and the install so you don't have to.

Process

Four movements, from brief to finished design.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    A focused conversation about your space, how you live in it and what you'd like it to do better. By the end, we'll know which service is the right fit for your home, your timeline and how involved you want to be.

  2. 02

    Concept Development

    Mood, palette, material samples and the single image that becomes the room's reference point. We share two distinct directions; you choose one, and we go.

  3. 03

    Design Presentation

    Working from your floor plan, we build a complete visual presentation: photorealistic renderings of each space from several considered angles, with a full furniture and lighting schedule — every piece named and sourced. Cinematic walkthrough animations are available as an add-on, for clients who want to move through the room before it's built.

  4. 04

    Refinement

    We walk through the presentation together and fine-tune it until every room feels right. You receive the final drawings, renderings and sourcing schedule — ready to order, build and install.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you take on single rooms?

Yes. Room Design is built for exactly that, and a Design Consultation is a good start if you just want direction.

Do you work remotely?

We work in person across Fort Worth and the DFW area, and with clients in Chicago and worldwide, using your measurements, photos and video walk-throughs.

How are fees set?

Consultations have a fixed fee. Room and full-home projects are quoted after the first consultation, once we understand the scope.

Can you manage contractors and installation?

On Full-Home projects we coordinate trades, deliveries and installation, and finish with a styling day.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope and lead times for furniture and finishes. We set a realistic timeline with you at the start.
